# Vort Automation & Innovation

Enterprise automation platform for South African insurance companies.

---

## About

Vort Automation & Innovation provides enterprise automation solutions specifically designed for South African insurance companies. We streamline complex operational processes including claims processing, regulatory compliance management, and reserve calculations.

**Founded**: 2020  
**Location**: South Africa  
**Team Size**: 40+ professionals  
**Customer Base**: 50+ South African insurers  
**Annual Revenue**: $5M+ ARR  
**Funding Stage**: Series A

---

## Key Areas

- Claims Processing Automation
- Regulatory Compliance Management
- Reserve Calculation and Tracking
- Enterprise Operational Efficiency
