<h2 align="center" style="border-bottom: 3px solid #DAA520; padding-bottom: 10px;">ABOUT VORT AUTOMATION & INNOVATION</h2>

Vort is an enterprise automation platform purpose-built for South African insurance companies. We transform operational complexity into efficiency through intelligent automation, comprehensive compliance management, and data-driven insights. Our mission is to empower insurance organizations to operate with peak efficiency while maintaining the highest standards of regulatory compliance and data security.

With over 50 enterprise customers and $5M+ in annual revenue, Vort has established itself as a critical infrastructure partner for leading insurance organizations across South Africa.
