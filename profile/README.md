## About The Organization

Vort Automation & Innovation is an enterprise automation platform built specifically for South African insurance companies. We streamline operational processes, ensure regulatory compliance, and enable organizations to operate with peak efficiency.

**Our Focus:**
- Claims processing automation
- Regulatory compliance management
- Reserve calculation and tracking
- Enterprise operational efficiency
