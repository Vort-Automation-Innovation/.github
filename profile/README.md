# Vort Automation & Innovation

Vort is a fairly new company (founded 2025) actively in communication with clients across South African insurance organizations. We're building purpose-built automation and compliance solutions designed specifically for the insurance industry.

**Company Status:** Pre-seed stage, actively in client engagement and product development

**Our Mission:** Empower insurance organizations through intelligent automation and continuous innovation

**What We're Building:**
- Enterprise automation for insurance operations  
- Regulatory compliance management solutions
- Data-driven operational insights
- Transforming manual processes into intelligent workflows

**Our Commitment:** We're transparent about where we are in our journey - a fairly new company working closely with clients to build solutions that address real insurance industry challenges.
