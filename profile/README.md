## 🎯 About Vort

Vort Automation & Innovation is an enterprise automation platform for South African insurance companies. We streamline claims, ensure compliance, and empower teams.

### 🌟 Our Mission

Empower organizations through intelligent automation and continuous innovation.

### 💼 What We Do

- **Automation** - Claims processing, reserving, policy management
- **Compliance** - Regulatory adherence and automated checks
- **Innovation** - Continuous improvements and new features
- **Support** - Enterprise-grade support for insurers
